Yunxiang Guo

AI Intern, World Trade Organization · Geneva, Switzerland

AI agent safety & applied ML

Geneva, Switzerland

guo_yx@outlook.com

I work at the intersection of AI agent safety, agentic AI, and applied machine learning — building AI systems that are not only capable, but auditable, aligned, and trustworthy under real-world constraints. My background spans deep reinforcement learning, human-in-the-loop AI system design, and quantitative finance. I completed my Master’s degree, majoring in Artificial Intelligence with a minor in Banking and Finance, at the Department of Informatics, University of Zurich, and was an exchange student at ETH Zurich.

Currently, I am an AI Intern at the World Trade Organization (Integrated Database Unit, Economic Research and Statistics Division), where I build LLM-assisted data pipelines and contribute to responsible AI solution design — schema intelligence, anomaly detection, prompt/model validation, and human-in-the-loop review for complex multilingual official statistics. Previously, as Junior Artificial Intelligence Officer at the International Labour Organization (UN), I led development of LLM multi-agent and RAG-based systems for labour market intelligence, working closely on the reliability, oversight, and responsible deployment of agentic AI in a policy-sensitive setting. In January 2026, I joined a roundtable at AI House Davos during World Economic Forum week, discussing the role of agentic AI in the public sector.

My research roots are in reinforcement learning alignment: at the ETH Zurich AI Center, I led a team combining numerical rewards with human preference feedback to align RL agent behavior with intended human goals — early hands-on work in what is now broadly known as AI alignment and agent safety. This carried into my Master’s thesis on risk-aware, CVaR-based reinforcement learning for financial decision-making, co-supervised by Prof. Dr. Josef Teichmann (Head of the Mathematics Department, ETH Zurich) and Prof. Dr. Claudio J. Tessone (Chairman of the UZH Blockchain Center).

Prior to this, I completed double Bachelor’s degrees: one in Information Engineering at the China University of Geosciences (Wuhan) (CUG) and the other in Business Administration at the Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). At CUG, I was supervised by Prof. Dr. Wenhao Yu (Head of the Department of Space Information). My second degree at HUST was supervised by Prof. Dr. Yingjun Zhu.

Full details are on my CV and Publications pages.

selected publications

  1. Impact of high-speed rail on urban economic development: an observation from the Beijing-Guangzhou line based on night-time light images
    Yunxiang Guo, Wenhao Yu, Zhuo Chen, and 1 more author
    Socio-Economic Planning Sciences, 2020
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    Using night-time light (NTL) data, this paper shows that the Beijing–Guangzhou High-Speed Railway significantly boosted economic growth in first-tier and new-first-tier cities along the corridor, while negatively affecting some second- and third-tier cities, reshaping the spatial economic structure along the corridor.

This paper evaluates the long-term economic impact of the Beijing–Guangzhou High-Speed Railway (BGHSR) on cities along the rail line using night-time light (NTL) data, as part of the College Students' Innovative Entrepreneurial Training Plan Program at the China National Engineering Research Center for Geographic Information System, under Prof. Dr. Wenhao Yu. The results reveal that the BGHSR significantly boosted economic growth in first-tier and new-first-tier cities but negatively impacted some second- and third-tier cities, reshaping the spatial economic structure along the corridor.

### About Me

I work at the intersection of **AI agent safety**, agentic AI, and applied machine learning — building AI systems that are not only capable, but auditable, aligned, and trustworthy under real-world constraints. My background spans deep reinforcement learning, human-in-the-loop AI system design, and quantitative finance. I completed my Master's degree, majoring in Artificial Intelligence with a minor in Banking and Finance, at the [Department of Informatics](https://www.ifi.uzh.ch/en.html), [University of Zurich](https://www.uzh.ch/en.html), and was an exchange student at [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html).

Currently, I am an **AI Intern** at the **World Trade Organization** (Integrated Database Unit, Economic Research and Statistics Division), where I build LLM-assisted data pipelines and contribute to responsible AI solution design — schema intelligence, anomaly detection, prompt/model validation, and human-in-the-loop review for complex multilingual official statistics. Previously, as **Junior Artificial Intelligence Officer** at the **International Labour Organization (UN)**, I led development of LLM multi-agent and RAG-based systems for labour market intelligence, working closely on the reliability, oversight, and responsible deployment of agentic AI in a policy-sensitive setting. In January 2026, I joined a roundtable at **AI House Davos** during World Economic Forum week, discussing the role of agentic AI in the public sector.

My research roots are in reinforcement learning alignment: at the **ETH Zurich AI Center**, I led a team combining numerical rewards with human preference feedback to align RL agent behavior with intended human goals — early hands-on work in what is now broadly known as AI alignment and agent safety. This carried into my Master's thesis on risk-aware, CVaR-based reinforcement learning for financial decision-making, co-supervised by [Prof. Dr. Josef Teichmann](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~jteichma/) (Head of the Mathematics Department, ETH Zurich) and [Prof. Dr. Claudio J. Tessone](https://www.blockchain.uzh.ch/members/prof-dr-claudio-j-tessone/) (Chairman of the [UZH Blockchain Center](https://www.blockchain.uzh.ch/)), as well as my Master's project supervised by Prof. Tessone and [Prof. Dr. Patrick Cheridito](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~patrickc/) ([Director of RiskLab Switzerland](https://risklab.ethz.ch/) and [Director of ETH's FinsureTech Hub](https://finsuretech.ethz.ch/)).

Prior to this, I completed double Bachelor's degrees: one in Information Engineering at the [China University of Geosciences (Wuhan) (CUG)](https://en.cug.edu.cn/) and the other in Business Administration at the [Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)](https://english.hust.edu.cn/). At CUG, I was supervised by [Prof. Dr. Wenhao Yu](https://grzy.cug.edu.cn/yuwenhao/en/index.htm) (Head of the Department of Space Information). My second degree at HUST was supervised by [Prof. Dr. Yingjun Zhu](http://english.cm.hust.edu.cn/info/1094/1299.htm).

Full details are on my [CV](/cv/) and [Publications](/publications/) pages.
